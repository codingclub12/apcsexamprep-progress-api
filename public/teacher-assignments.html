Assignments

green is assigned, grey is locked

A switch on a unit sets the whole unit. A switch on a lesson sets that lesson and beats its unit. A switch on one assignment beats both. So the usual shape is to lock a unit, then open the lesson you are teaching this week, and the rest stays shut without you listing it.

A half-filled switch means the things under it disagree, which is normal once you have opened one lesson inside a locked unit. Clicking it settles everything under it one way. A switch shown in italics is inherited: nothing is set at that level, so it is following the level above it. Setting it explicitly PINS it, which is what you want for an assignment that must stay open even if you lock the unit later.
