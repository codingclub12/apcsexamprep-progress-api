How to read this

This page renders the canonical gradebook contract, and it calls the same builder the teacher route calls with the same arguments. It is not a second implementation of the teacher's gradebook: it is that gradebook, so what you verify here is what they see. Names are hidden by default because the numbers can be checked without them.

The grade is earned over graded: points scored, over the points of the work actually attempted. It is never divided by the whole course, because a student who has finished three items perfectly is at 100 percent, not 3 percent. How much of the course has been reached is pace, reported separately. A blank cell means not attempted. A 0 means attempted and scored zero.
