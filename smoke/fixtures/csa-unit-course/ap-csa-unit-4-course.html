What you'll learn in Unit 4
  • The ethical and social implications of collecting, storing, and processing data at scale
  • How to create, index, traverse, and apply algorithms to 1D arrays of primitives and objects
  • How to use ArrayList methods and traverse ArrayLists with for and for-each loops
  • How to declare, create, and traverse 2D arrays using nested loops
  • How linear search, binary search, selection sort, and insertion sort work and compare
  • How to read data from text files using File and Scanner
  • How to trace recursive methods and identify base and recursive cases
