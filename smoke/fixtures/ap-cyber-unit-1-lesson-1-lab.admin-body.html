Lab: Phishing Email Dissection

You are a junior security analyst reviewing 4 suspicious emails flagged by your organization’s email gateway. For each email, identify the attack indicators, classify the tactics used, and assess the risk level.

🔬 Hands-On Lab Skills 1.A, 1.B, 1.C ~40–50 min 4 Email Specimens
Lab Progress: 0 / 4 Emails Analyzed 0 / 24 pts

Scoring Rubric

Each email analysis is worth 6 points. Review the criteria before you begin.

Category Points What You Need
Sender Verification 1 pt Correctly identify what is suspicious about the sender address or display name
Psychological Tactic 1 pt Identify whether the message uses intimidation, urgency, both, or neither
Suspicious Elements 1 pt Identify at least 2 suspicious elements in the email body
Victim Impact 1 pt Correctly classify what the victim would give up: personal information, secure information, malware or captured credentials, or none of the three
Potential Impact 1 pt Identify what the adversary is trying to obtain or achieve
Recommended Action 1 pt Describe what the recipient should do

AP Exam Tip: Every message in this lab asks two things: which psychological tactic the adversary used, and what the victim stood to lose. Topic 1.1 names exactly two tactics, intimidation and urgency, and three kinds of impact. Labels like spear phishing, vishing, and smishing are not part of Topic 1.1 and are not assessed. Read senders character by character, hover links before clicking, and verify any request for money, credentials, or codes through a separate channel you look up yourself.
