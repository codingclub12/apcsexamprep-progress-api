APCSExamPrep • full-year pacing, materials & your teaching progress
Pilot (for use beginning 2026-2027 school year). A suggested pace in 45-minute periods: on a block (90-minute) schedule, merge two days into one block. Adjust to your calendar and the day ranges update automatically. The 22 topics plus each unit's performance task, review, and test come to 66 periods; with the 3-period course launch that is the 69-period core. The full-year guide reaches 180 periods by adding labeled extension blocks (lab, certification crosswalk, capstone, and flex) rather than padding the core. Teacher packs open in Drive; lesson buttons open the live student pages. Your "taught" progress is saved on this device.
