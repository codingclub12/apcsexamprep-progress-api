About Tanner Crow and APCSExamPrep

Tanner Crow AP Computer Science Teacher, Blue Valley North High School My Wyzant tutoring profile

I'm Tanner Crow. I teach AP Computer Science at Blue Valley North High School, and I built APCSExamPrep.com and still run it myself.

Who I am and what I teach

I have taught AP Computer Science at Blue Valley North for over eleven years. I also tutor students one on one over Zoom in AP Computer Science A, AP Computer Science Principles and AP Cybersecurity, and AP CSA is the course people ask me for most.

Why I built this site

I built this site for my own students before I opened it to other classrooms.

Most of what is here is free: the practice exams, the study guides, the daily practice questions, the study games, the AP CSA free-response archive and the whole AP Cybersecurity course. The Question of the Day, the topic practice tests and the FRQ archive were built to back up what I cover in tutoring sessions.

What is here

There are four AP courses on the site.

AP Computer Science A

Java programming, following the current four-unit course. The exam prep hub has full practice exams, the unit study guides and past AP CSA free-response questions going back to 2004.

AP Computer Science Principles

The courseAP CSP resources

AP Cybersecurity

The complete course, five units of lessons and labs. The practice page adds full free-response sets in the exam's Device Security Analysis format, plus terminal labs.

The courseAP Cybersecurity practice

AP Networking

Four units. Every topic has its own study guide and quiz, and every unit has a lab that runs in the browser. College Board is piloting the course in 2026-27, and it opens to all schools in 2027-28.

The course

For teachers

If you teach one of these courses, the teacher dashboard is free. You create a class and give your students one code. They join with a name and a PIN, no email address needed, and you can see where each of them is, lesson by lesson.

Set up a class

If you need a quiz by Friday, the AP CSA Test Builder and the AP CSP Test Builder let you pick questions by unit, topic and difficulty and print a PDF exam with a separate answer key. No login required.

Tutoring
