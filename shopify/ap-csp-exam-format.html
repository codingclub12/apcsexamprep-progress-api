AP Computer Science Principles Exam Format (2027)

The AP CSP score comes from two places: a 70-question multiple-choice exam in May, and a program you build in class and then write about on exam day. Here is how each part works, when it happens, and what it is worth.

Exam day

Create task due
Fri, Apr 30, 202711:59 PM Eastern

The exam is digital. The end-of-course exam, both the multiple choice and the written responses, is taken in College Board's Bluebook app, and your answers submit on their own when time runs out. The Create task is submitted separately, through the AP Digital Portfolio, two weeks before exam day.

College Board keeps the question types and weights the same every year, so what you practice now is what you will see in May.

Section I: Multiple choice

All 70 questions have four answer choices, but they are not all the same kind of question:

Question typeHow manyWhat it looks like
Single select57Pick the one correct answer.
Single select with a reading passage5Read a short passage about a computing innovation, then answer questions about it.
Multiple select8Pick exactly two answers.

That is 57 + 5 + 8 = 70. On the multiple-select questions, look for the instruction to choose two. It is easy to miss when you are moving fast.

Pacing

120 minutes for 70 questions works out to a little over a minute and a half per question. Code-tracing questions take longer than vocabulary questions, so bank time on the quick ones. The multiple-choice pacing strategy goes through how to split the time.

Section II: The Create performance task

The Create task is a program you design and build yourself, on any topic you choose. You get 9 hours of class time to finish three things:

Your program code

The complete program, which you submit along with a video of it running.

Your Personalized Project Reference

Screenshots of specific parts of your code. You get this back on exam day to answer the written responses, so it has to be readable.

Everything is submitted as final in the AP Digital Portfolio by 11:59 PM Eastern on Friday, April 30, 2027. Your teacher schedules the 9 hours of class time.

Section II: The written responses

On exam day, after the multiple choice, you get 60 minutes to answer 2 questions about your own program. You can see your Personalized Project Reference the whole time. The 2 questions break into 4 prompts, and each prompt comes from one category:

PromptCategoryBe ready to explain
Written Response 1Program design, function and purposeWhat your program is for, what it does, its inputs and outputs, and how you developed and tested it.
Written Response 2(a)Algorithm developmentHow an algorithm in your code works: its conditions, its loops, and what would change if you modified it.
Written Response 2(b)Errors and testingFinding and fixing an error, and choosing inputs and expected outputs that would show your code works.
Written Response 2(c)Data and procedural abstractionHow your list stores data, how you move through it, and how your procedure makes the program easier to manage.

The prompts ask about your code, not code in general, so the best preparation is knowing your own program well enough to explain any part of it.

Timeline for the 2026-27 school year

  1. Through the year: learn the five Big Ideas. The multiple choice covers the whole course. Work through the full AP CSP course or the study guides.
  2. When your teacher schedules it: 9 hours for the Create task. Build the program, record the video, and make your Personalized Project Reference.
  3. By Friday, April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM Eastern: submit the Create task as final in the AP Digital Portfolio.
  4. Friday, May 14, 2027, Session 1: the end-of-course exam. 70 multiple-choice questions, then the 2 written responses. For most schools, including all of the lower 48 states, Hawaii and Washington D.C., Session 1 starts at 8 a.m. local time.

What you have on exam day

  • Your Personalized Project Reference, for the written responses.
  • The exam reference sheet, inside Bluebook and also printed and mailed to schools. Learn it before May so you are not reading it for the first time.
  • A test preview in Bluebook. Download the app early and try the preview so the tools are not new on the day.

Questions

When is the 2027 AP Computer Science Principles exam?

Friday, May 14, 2027, in Session 1, which is the morning slot. The Create performance task has to be submitted as final by 11:59 PM Eastern on Friday, April 30, 2027.

How many questions are on the AP CSP exam?

70 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes, then 2 written-response questions in 60 minutes. The 70 are 57 single-select questions, 5 single-select questions with a reading passage, and 8 multiple-select questions where you pick two answers.

Is the AP CSP exam digital?

Yes. The end-of-course exam is fully digital in the Bluebook app, and responses are submitted automatically at the end. The Create task is submitted separately in the AP Digital Portfolio.

How much is the Create task worth?

The Create task and the 2 written responses about it make up Section II, which is 30% of your score. The multiple choice is the other 70%.

Can I see my code during the written responses?

You get your Personalized Project Reference, the code screenshots you submitted with your Create task. That is why it has to be clear and readable, and why choosing what goes in it matters.

Is there a reference sheet on the AP CSP exam?

Yes. The exam reference sheet is available in Bluebook and is also printed and mailed to schools. It explains the pseudocode used in the questions.

Should I take AP CSP or AP CSA?

AP CSP covers the broad ideas of computing and uses pseudocode on the exam. AP CSA is Java programming and goes deeper into writing code. Many students take CSP first. See AP CSA vs AP CSP for the full comparison.
