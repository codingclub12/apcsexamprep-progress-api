## The files

The bundle is 100 documents in Google Drive. Eighty-eight of them are lesson
materials, four per topic, across all 22 topics:

- **Teacher deck.** Speaker notes and the answer reveals built into the slides.
- **Student deck.** The same slides with the notes and reveals taken out.
- **Teacher guide.** Answer key, timing, facilitation notes, and the
  misconceptions students actually walk in with.
- **Student guide.** Un-keyed guided notes for students to fill in.

Topics are 1.1 through 1.4, then 2.1 through 2.6, 3.1 through 3.6, and 4.1
through 4.6.

The remaining twelve are the assessments, three per unit:

- **Unit test.** Unit 1 is 16 multiple choice, Units 2, 3 and 4 are 24 each.
  Every test also carries two free response prompts.
- **Answer key** for that test.
- **Performance task** with its scoring rubric. The four are the Device Recovery
  Walkthrough, the Lab Network Field Manual, the Game Day Network Blueprint, and
  the Incident Report and Resilience Plan. Each one grows out of the scenario its
  unit opens with rather than sitting beside it.

Everything is .pptx and .docx. Edit any of it, cut what you do not want.

## The online half

Your students work the 22 topic pages on the site. Each page has a practice
activity and an 8 question quiz. Both grade themselves, and both reshuffle their
cases and answer order on every attempt, so a retake is not the same test twice.

Four browser labs sit on top of that, one per unit: a device triage bench, a
small office documentation and handoff, a segmented LAN build with routing and
filtering, and a capture and trace investigation. Each one runs a simulated
network with a working command line, so ping, tracert, ipconfig, arp and nslookup
all answer from the same model. Nothing to install. They run on a Chromebook.

Scores land in your gradebook as students work. You set the mastery threshold and
whether retries count, and changing either one re-scores the work already in
there instead of making you start again.
